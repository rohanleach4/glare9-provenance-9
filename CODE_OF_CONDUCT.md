# Code of Conduct

Provenance•9 contributors must communicate professionally, discuss ideas on their merits and make room for differing experience and perspectives. Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate humiliation, doxxing and publication of another person’s confidential information are not acceptable.

Security reports, customer data, credentials and private operational evidence must not be posted publicly. Follow `SECURITY.md` for vulnerability reporting.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove material that violates these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. A concern about public project conduct may be raised privately with the repository owner. Reports should identify the conduct, location, date and any immediate safety concern; knowingly false or retaliatory reports are also unacceptable.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately and avoid unnecessary disclosure about affected people. This code applies in repository discussions, reviews and other spaces where someone is representing the project.
